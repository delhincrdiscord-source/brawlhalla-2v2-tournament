What Is Brawlhalla 2v2?

⚔️

Double Elimination

Lose once and you're not out — you drop to the losers bracket and fight your way back. Only two losses send you home. Grand final with bracket reset.

🛡️

Team Sign-Up

One player registers the duo. Enter Discord IDs, in-game names, and your current & peak 2v2 elo. We track both players so nobody plays on two teams.

🏆

Seeded by Skill

Teams are seeded by average current elo, so the strongest squads enter on opposite sides of the bracket. Brackets are generated automatically.

Know Before You Brawl

Rules & Format

01

Registration: one player submits for the team. If either Discord ID or in-game username is already registered, the system blocks the duplicate — no double-teaming.

02

Format: 2v2 double elimination. Winners bracket, losers bracket, grand final. If the losers-bracket champion takes game one of the grand final, we run a full bracket reset.

03

Matches: best-of-3 until semifinals; best-of-5 from semifinals onward. Legend & weapon bans announced by the TO before each round.

04

Schedule: all rounds are played on the Discord server. Check the bracket page after every round — match results go live the moment the TO reports them.
